# MLS remarks and six days of social

## MLS public remarks

Built from verified facts only. As long as the facts allow, never padded to MetroList's 2,500 characters. Count the characters and report the count.

Order, one short paragraph each, skip any paragraph the facts do not support:

1. The price and why. Name the comps with month and price. "Priced under the comps on purpose. The last three homes on this exact [BUILDER] floor plan closed at [P1] in [M1], [P2] in [M2] and [P3] in [M3], per [COUNTY] County records. This one is listed at [PRICE]."
2. The numbers. Beds, baths, square feet, lot, year, HOA or no HOA, what the street is (court, cul de sac, through street).
3. Layout. Ground floor square feet if known. What you see when you walk in.
4. Kitchen, honestly. If it is original, say so and say the price reflects it.
5. Bedrooms and baths.
6. Systems with years, only the ones on the record or the disclosures.
7. Outside.
8. Where it is. Nearby shopping and freeway access by name. School district with "confirm school assignments with the district."
9. The source line and the close. "Square footage and lot size are from county records. If you have been watching [NEIGHBORHOOD] for a [BEDS] bedroom under [ROUND_PRICE], see this one first."

Never: "perfect for families", "safe", "quiet neighborhood", "great schools", anything about who should live there, anything about the sellers, "motivated", "must see", "won't last".

The worked example, 1,879 characters, is in the Launch My Listing page under "The words, already written".

## Six days of social

One post a day for the six days after launch. Each post is one photo and two to four plain sentences in the agent's voice. Call the photo out by its number in the MLS set so the agent can find it. End each with the short URL. No hashtags walls, no emojis, no "DM me".

- Day 1, launch. The front of the house, the price, the one line on the comps.
- Day 2, the thing you cannot add later. The lot, the court, the view, the aerial. "This is the court. No through traffic, one way in, one way out. That's the thing you can't add to a house later."
- Day 3, the room you walk into. What the agent noticed standing there.
- Day 4, the honest one. The kitchen or whatever is original, and why the price reflects it.
- Day 5, open house reminder. Day, hours, walk in.
- Day 6, the neighborhood. One named thing nearby and how far.

After the open house, two more if the agent wants them: the visitor count on Monday, and the sold post the week it closes.
