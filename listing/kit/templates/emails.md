# The four emails

They send from BoldTrail in the agent's name. Fill every bracket from the verified config. A bracket left in sends as a bracket, so 03 and 04 are delivered to the agent with the brackets still in and a note saying when to fill them.

Voice: the agent talking to someone who already knows them. Short sentences. One ask per email. No "I'm thrilled", no "stunning", no "don't miss". Describe the house, never the people. Phone numbers with hyphens. No em dashes or en dashes.

Every email ends with the agent's signature block from the roster:

[AGENT_NAME]
[AGENT_TITLE], DRE #[AGENT_DRE]
Weichert, Realtors - Sierra Pacific Group
[AGENT_CELL] · [AGENT_EMAIL]
You are getting this because we have talked about buying or selling. Reply "stop" and I will take you off.

---

## 01 Just listed (send launch day, the day the listing is filed)

Subject: [ADDRESS_SHORT] is listed at [PRICE]

[FIRST_NAME],

I just listed [ADDRESS_SHORT] in [NEIGHBORHOOD], [CITY]. [BEDS] bedrooms, [BATHS] baths, [SQFT] square feet, [ONE_FACT_FROM_THE_RECORD, e.g. "built in 2002, no HOA, on a court"].

It is priced at [PRICE]. [ONE_SENTENCE_ON_THE_PRICE_FROM_THE_COMPS, e.g. "The last three on this plan closed between $750,000 and $770,000, so this one is under the cluster on purpose."]

[THE_ONE_THING_NOTICED, in the agent's words, one or two sentences.]

Open house [OH1_DAY] [OH1_DATE], [OH1_HOURS][, and OH2_DAY OH2_DATE, OH2_HOURS]. Walk in, no appointment.

Everything is here, photos included: [SITE_URL]

If you know someone looking for [BEDS] bedrooms in [CITY], send them the link. If that someone is you, text me.

[SIGNATURE]

## 02 Open house (send the morning of the first open house, or the day before)

Subject: Open [OH1_DAY] and [OH2_DAY], [ADDRESS_SHORT]

[FIRST_NAME],

[ADDRESS_SHORT] is open [OH1_DAY] [OH1_HOURS][ and OH2_DAY OH2_HOURS]. I am there the whole time with the disclosures in hand.

[PHOTO_CALLOUT, e.g. "Photo 7 is the living room. The ceiling goes up two stories and that is the first thing you see when you walk in."]

[OFFER_REVIEW_LINE if a date is published: "Offers are reviewed [OFFER_REVIEW_DATE]. If you want to write, see it this weekend." Otherwise: "Priced at [PRICE], under what the last ones closed for. Come see why."]

Directions and the calendar link: [SITE_URL]

Can't make it? Text me and I will find you a time.

[SIGNATURE]

---

## 03 Monday recap (send Monday after the open house; fill the number Sunday night)

Subject: What happened at [ADDRESS_SHORT] this weekend

[FIRST_NAME],

[VISITORS] people came through [ADDRESS_SHORT] over the weekend. [ONE_HONEST_LINE: what they asked about, what they liked, or "The kitchen question came up every time, and every time the answer was the price."]

[STATUS_LINE: "Offers are reviewed [OFFER_REVIEW_DATE]." or "It is still available." or "We have offers in hand and the sellers are reviewing them."]

If you have been thinking about what your own place would bring, this weekend was a live comp. Reply with your address and I will run the same numbers for you. No obligation, no follow-up parade.

[SIGNATURE]

---

## 04 Just sold (send the week it closes; fill the price when it records)

Subject: [ADDRESS_SHORT] sold for [SOLD_PRICE]

[FIRST_NAME],

[ADDRESS_SHORT] closed at [SOLD_PRICE], [DAYS_ON_MARKET] days after it listed. [ONE_LINE_ON_HOW: "Listed at [PRICE], [N] offers, closed over asking." or "Listed at [PRICE] and sold at the number."]

That is the newest closed sale in [NEIGHBORHOOD], which means it is now the comp for every house around it.

If you own one nearby and want to know what this does to your number, reply with your address. I will send you the math, not a pitch.

[SIGNATURE]
